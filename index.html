Frequently Asked Questions

How accurate is the Gemini AI crop disease detection?

AgroSaathi uses Google’s Gemini multimodal vision model. It delivers high diagnosis accuracy across common Indian crops including Rice, Wheat, Cotton, Tomato, Potato, Sugarcane, and Pulses.

Is AgroSaathi available in Hindi and Gujarati?

Yes. Use the language selector in the menu to switch between English, हिंदी and ગુજરાતી. The AI Assistant also replies in the language you ask in.

Do I need to pay to use the Crop Disease Scan or AI Assistant?

No. Crop Disease Scan and the AI Assistant are free for farmers.

How do I sell my produce in the Marketplace?

Register, verify your farmer profile, then list your crop with quantity and price. Buyers contact you directly, with no middleman fee.

What if I don’t have an internet camera connected?

You can upload a leaf photo from your phone gallery, or describe the symptoms to the AI Assistant in text.
